# About AuthFlow ### Technology made easier to understand. AuthFlow is an educational web project designed to demonstrate the fundamentals of web authentication and interactive computing. The purpose of this website is to help students and visitors understand how modern websites manage user accounts, authentication and digital logic through practical demonstrations. ### What does this website offer? **AuthFlow** - Email and password authentication. - Google sign-in option. - Password recovery. - A personalized dashboard. - A responsive and interactive user interface. **Logic Lab** - Interactive simulation of seven digital logic gates. - Truth tables for understanding gate operations. - Number-system conversion between binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal. ### Our Teachers, Our Inspiration This project was made possible with the guidance, encouragement and cooperation of our teachers. Their valuable support helped us understand the concepts, plan the project and bring our ideas into practice.
